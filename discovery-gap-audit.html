Why the Conservatory was missing

  • Captured, then stalled. Its Wedding Spot link entered the saved queue on October 26. Its official pages were never reviewed and it never reached Compare.
  • The counts hid the gap. Thousands of room records and many search passes did not establish property coverage.
  • Corrected: Conservatory, its Atrium, and The Hibernia are now listed from official wedding pages. More candidates still need review.

Capture every lead→Check identity + location→Verify event permission→List, exclude with reason, or flag pending

How discovery will work
  • Work by county, municipality and venue category; track each source inventory and pagination separately.
  • Cross-check wedding directories against local venue associations, tourism inventories, official public rentals, planners, caterers and photographers.
  • Keep property, room, alias and package separate. Match addresses and operators; never merge on similar names alone.
  • Keep every closing time and price range. Midnight is only an optional personal wedding preference.
  • Give every captured profile a dated state: pending, added, existing, excluded or blocked. Downloading a page is not reviewing it.
  • Verify unknown permission before calling a retreat, restaurant or camp a wedding venue. Preserve possible spaces in the separate view.
  • Publish unresolved leads and measure properties reviewed, source inventories reconciled, and remaining gaps. Never claim an exhaustive list from a fixed number of loops.
