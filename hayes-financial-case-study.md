Checked October 11, 2026. This is a historical hotel/conference-center case, not a current valuation or wedding-only business forecast.

The audit records a $30 million contractual base consideration for improved real property plus related rights, personal and intangible assets, subject to inventory/receivable/closing adjustments. Sale finalized February 4, 2019; city ownership/operation ended effective February 5. Asset allocation and final net proceeds remain unverified. Do not label this as a $30 million property-only value or add a separate business valuation.

The operating statements exclude buildings, improvements and related debt held elsewhere in city accounts. Accounting operating income therefore does not establish investment return or current NOI. No annualization or capitalization is applied. [Original city audit, pages 4–6 and 12](https://www.sanjoseca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/39186/637049316526000000).

The operator dates mansion completion to 1905 and conference-center opening to 1994. Neither establishes wedding-opening date or current operator tenure. [Operator history](https://www.hayesmansion.com/our-story/history/).
