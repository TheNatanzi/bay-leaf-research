# Permission to hold a wedding versus operate a venue
Source check: October 11, 2026. This is a research screen, not a property-specific approval. Record the actual jurisdiction, parcel and permit conditions before treating a candidate as feasible.

| Question | Evidence to request |
|---|---|
| Can this exact property host our wedding? | Written confirmation of ceremony/reception use, rooms/grounds, guest layout, date and all deadlines. |
| Can Bay Leaf operate recurring paid weddings here? | Current land-use entitlement, allowed annual event count, operator/ownership conditions, commercial lease or concession rights, and any required amendments. |
| Does outside catering remove venue responsibilities? | Health department classification, food-service infrastructure/plan check, licensed caterer, water/waste/handwashing and permitted alcohol-service plan. |
| Can music run to midnight? | Separate indoor/outdoor amplified-sound limits, permit conditions, contract cutoff and cleanup deadline. An access window is insufficient. |

## Sonoma County: verified current periodic-event pathway
Permit Sonoma's current guidance confirms that the Board adopted the update December 9, 2025, effective June 9, 2026. Its linked consolidated code, displayed as the September 25, 2026 current version, includes the adopted amendments in section 26-22-120. This resolves the earlier draft-versus-adopted verification gap. [Current Permit Sonoma guidance](https://permitsonoma.org/specialeventpermits) · [Current zoning code, section 26-22-120](https://library.municode.com/ca/sonoma_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=CH26SOCOZORE_ART22REEDPUASUSST_S26-22-120PESPEV).

The periodic-event pathway allows up to four events on a site in two years. Events at least 30 days apart require separate zoning permits. The attendee cap is 2,500 at any one time, with a specified public-right-of-way/encroachment-permit exception. The code also excludes qualifying events entirely within appropriately permitted buildings and events within the approved use of fairgrounds/outdoor spectator facilities. **Do not apply the occasional-event limit to every approved venue without examining its actual entitlement.** The earlier county staff report separately says winery agricultural promotional standards are unaffected. [County staff report](https://sonoma-county.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=3795E6F1-7701-4414-AB81-C8BB81143BC1&ID=7768001).

For Bay Leaf, the occasional-event allowance alone cannot support a recurring wedding business. Check the parcel's approved use and commercial operating rights first. The code's agricultural-district restrictions and other applicable approvals remain separate. A public rental or wedding advertisement does not establish permission to lease and operate the property commercially.

The current application guidance requests submissions at least 60 days before an event. Noise follows the county general plan; the 7 AM–11 PM window in the code is one condition of a narrow exclusion, **not a universal licensed venue cutoff**. Music, overnight accommodations, food, tents and associated activities may trigger additional review. The code requires neighborhood notice at least ten days before the event. State and county regional parks use their own agencies' event-permit pathways rather than Permit Sonoma's. Property-specific permission, event frequency and actual indoor/outdoor deadlines remain Unknown until verified.

Evidence checked October 11, 2026 UTC (October 10 Pacific): current agency page and consolidated code reviewed in the live browser; displayed text saved locally. The final signed ordinance document itself was not downloaded, but current agency confirmation and enacted codified text were verified.

## Napa County: temporary-event exemption does not authorize wedding use
The September 23, 2025 staff report on proposed temporary-event changes says weddings and private parties are outside that temporary-event category and fall under other county provisions. Winery marketing is separately defined and subordinate to wine production. This is not permission to turn an agricultural winery into a wedding business. [County report](https://napa.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=74AAFD7E-5036-4DBF-A49B-C4EDD4C68308&ID=7663685&Options=&Search=).

The newer August 25, 2026 agenda item (26-1526) and current county project page describe an updated proposal, with implementation listed for January 2027 if adopted. The agenda page still displays “Agenda Ready” with no final action. Do not use the older 2025 item as proof of adoption or treat the proposal as current operating permission. [2026 county agenda item](https://napa.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=35AABC94-AC02-4F2B-8EB9-032AE4766A10&ID=8185096&Options=&Search=) · [County project timeline](https://connect.napacounty.gov/temporary-event-ordinance).

Verification gap: final adoption/current code and each property's grandfathered or approved uses remain unverified. Distinguish unincorporated county land from incorporated cities; keep candidate wedding permission Unknown until property-specific evidence supports it.

## Marin County: identify the actual food-service model
Marin's guidance, updated May 19, 2026, requires a current annual caterer health permit. A host-facility permit applies to a defined model involving direct public sales and venue infrastructure. Its four-hours-in-twelve operational limit is not a blanket four-hour limit on all weddings. The page says that host permit is unnecessary when vendors use none of the venue kitchen, refrigeration, storage, warewashing or other food-service infrastructure. Zoning, fire and other food/alcohol requirements still need their own assessment. [Marin Environmental Health](https://www.marincounty.gov/departments/cda/env-health-svcs/prgm-food/catering-host-facilities).

## Berkeley: current application availability matters
As checked October 11, 2026, Berkeley's official page says new or expanded 2026 special-event applications are no longer accepted; returning/already-queued events are unaffected and 2027 applications are accepted. Its described pathway concerns city street/park events. This does not establish that all private indoor weddings or ordinary facility rentals are unavailable. [City event permits](https://berkeleyca.gov/city-services/event-permits-rentals/special-event-permits).
