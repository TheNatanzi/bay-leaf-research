Ask once. Save everything. Update the facts.

One conversation per property, covering all its rooms.

1 · Read first →Website, brochure, FAQ and existing emails
2 · Ask gaps →Short, personal introduction; full venue name
3 · Archive →Original email, attachments and linked documents
4 · UpdateDated facts, evidence links and tested columns
Conflicting answer? Keep both sources → ask one short clarification → update when resolved.

How the first email feels

Friendly introduction, a request for current information and two or three unanswered questions. No questionnaire dump.

Example: after reading a venue brochure

Hi there,

We’re exploring wedding venues and would love to learn more about [Full venue name]. I had a look through your rental information—could you share your current wedding pricing or a sample quote, including any required fees?

We’d also love to confirm when music needs to finish and how much time is allowed for cleanup.

Thank you,
[Assigned sender]

What gets saved
EvidencePermanent record
EmailOriginal .eml, readable text, message/thread IDs
AttachmentOriginal bytes, filename, date and SHA-256
Linked brochureDated snapshot, original URL and hash; failed downloads stay flagged
FactExact room/package, value, conditions, effective date and evidence ID

Original correspondence and attachments remain private. Only reviewed, non-confidential venue facts enter the public atlas.

Automatic checks and follow-ups
  • Match the parent property and all room/contact aliases before sending.
  • Check both research accounts and existing wedding correspondence for duplicates.
  • Stop follow-ups on replies, declines or opt-outs. A new answer becomes a clarification conversation.
  • Unknown mandatory charges keep the cost incomplete; deposits and guest reimbursements are separate.
  • Scheduled inbox checks run when the local app can execute them; this is not an always-on mail server.
