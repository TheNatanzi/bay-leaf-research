## Why the old method missed venues

1. **Captured leads were left unresolved.** Conservatory SF was found October 26 but not promoted until November 7.
2. **Source coverage was too narrow.** Caterer rosters, cultural institutions, restaurants, clubs and federal rentals exposed omissions.
3. **Buttons and documents were not consistently followed.** Fact sheets, packages and obligations contain details absent from landing pages.
4. **Counts hid unfinished review.** Room rows, directories downloaded and searches performed are different from properties reviewed.
5. **Names and dates needed stronger checks.** Rebrands, redirects, city namesakes, closed public attractions and expired lease notices can mislead.
