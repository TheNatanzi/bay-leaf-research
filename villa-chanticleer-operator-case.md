# Villa Chanticleer: operating an existing public venue

Checked October 26, 2026. This is a historical operating-agreement example, **not an available lease listing, a venue valuation or a profit estimate**. Selected clauses of the city's 52-page agreement and six-page staff report were reviewed; the signature page was visually checked. Later amendments and actual financial results have not been established.

## What the documents establish

| Question | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|
| Who owns the real estate? | The agreement identifies the City of Healdsburg as owner. Operating rights do not transfer land ownership. |
| When did this operator start? | Milestone's effective date is July 1, 2022. Earlier resort and operator history is separate from first wedding operation. |
| How long is the agreement? | Initial five-year term through June 30, 2027; one conditional five-year extension option with notice requirements. An option does not establish that renewal happened. |
| What does the operator give the city? | Section 2 describes consideration through specified free and discounted community rentals. Do not call this a free business: operating obligations remain substantial. |
| What does the operator handle? | Event delivery, staffing, administration, utilities, supplies, security/cleanup and specified upkeep, subject to the agreement's detailed allocation. |
| What does the city maintain? | Specified structural, major equipment and building components. Funding remains subject to public budgeting; responsibilities are not interchangeable with an ordinary private lease. |
| What financial evidence is missing? | The agreement requires an annual revenue/expense report. Actual reports, normalized earnings, assets/liabilities and transferable business value have not been verified here. |

## A useful contradiction

The staff report describes **six** waived Legion events annually; the signed agreement's §7.4(c) states **three**. Preserve both source versions and check amendments before relying on an obligation. Do not project revenue from a staff summary alone. The staff report's historical city maintenance budget and estimated community-benefit value are neither the operator's total expenses nor its profit.

## A current operating constraint

The city reports a **January 1–April 9, 2027 booking hold** for proposed kitchen, bar and entrance-accessibility work. Its stated **$1.5 million state grant** is project funding, not the property's market value or the operator's purchase price. Completion and revised booking dates need confirmation. [City modernization project](https://www.cityofhealdsburg.org/1200/Villa-Chanticleer-Modernization-Project).

The operator advertises a wedding offer for up to **250 guests**, base event time **4–10 PM**, and an extension toward midnight at **$500/hour**. This does not establish midnight music or a complete event price. The city's larger generic occupancy figures apply to different configurations. [Operator wedding offer](https://milestoneeventsgroup.com/venue/villa-chanticleer/).

## What this changes in Bay Leaf's feasibility work

- Add public operating agreements and concessions as a specific version of the **lease an established lawful venue** route. A one-day public room rental is a different transaction.
- Price replacement management labor, utilities, maintenance, community discounts and downtime. Land acquisition can be avoided while meaningful operating obligations remain.
- Check permitted use, assignment, renewal, capital-work allocation and inherited bookings before modeling an acquisition or operating-rights transfer.
- Keep property value, business value, capital grants, gross revenue and profit in separate fields. The evidence here supports no current appraisal or earnings valuation.

The other routes remain: convert a leased space, buy/develop land, or acquire an established venue/business. None inherits this agreement's economics automatically.
