Overnight stays: accommodation is an obligation before it is a saving

A room block, a required room minimum and an entire-property buyout are different commitments. Record rooms, permitted sleepers, required nights, complete room prices, payment responsibility and the deadline for releasing unbooked rooms.

  • Total commitment: all required rooms and nights, including mandatory lodging fees and taxes.
  • Confirmed offset: guest payments actually secured against that obligation.
  • Planned offset: additional guest contributions you expect but have not secured.
  • Your risk: the amount you must cover if guests do not book or reimburse you. Voluntary offsite hotels are not automatically your expense.
  • Guest burden: required per-person/per-room stay costs and travel; a low couple price may shift expense to guests.

A real example: Dawn Ranch's 2026 exclusive-weekend package specifies a buyout of all 85 accommodations, with room rates excluded from the venue fee. Its package also requires a full-service insured planner. Terms vary by package and must be reconfirmed. Read the operator's 2026 package.

Unexpected requirements to research for every shortlisted venue

Minimums + exclusivity

  • Room buyout, minimum nights and unbooked-room liability
  • Guest minimums and food/bar spend floors
  • Required rehearsal dinner, breakfast or welcome event
  • Exclusive caterer/bar and outside-vendor buyout fees
  • Late-night access tied to extra rooms or spaces

People + time

  • Required planner/coordinator
  • Security, parking attendants, valet or shuttles
  • Server/bartender minimum hours and headcounts
  • Setup, cleanup, overtime and late-night staffing
  • Vendor meals, travel and accommodation

Equipment + infrastructure

  • Tables/chairs versus linens, plates and glassware
  • Kitchen, prep area, refrigeration and waste removal
  • Sound, microphones, dance floor and lighting
  • Tent, flooring, rain backup, heat and power
  • Restrooms, accessibility and transportation

Charges + contract

  • Service/admin fees, tax bases and separate gratuity
  • Corkage, cake cutting, delivery and pickup charges
  • Insurance, required permits and damage costs
  • Deposits: refundable, credited or an extra charge
  • Cancellation, room-release and final-count deadlines

Track event end, music stop, alcohol stop, guest departure and cleanup separately. A standing capacity is not dinner-and-dancing capacity. Venue-tour questions and planner commentary. Sources checked October 25, 2026; proposals and workbook methods below are ours.

Suggested grade rules to discuss—not agreed defaults

The optional preview uses weighted 1–5 ratings converted to 0–100: 1 → 0, 3 → 50, 5 → 100. Proposed bands: A ≥ 90, B ≥ 80, C ≥ 70, D ≥ 60, F < 60. Change the bands if they do not match how you want to use grades.

Only selected preferences with positive weights contribute. Every selected weight needs both ratings before a final grade appears. Must-haves are checked separately. Missing facts do not become zero, and agreement about taste does not verify a venue's operating rules.
